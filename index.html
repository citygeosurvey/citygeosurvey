How We Work Remotely

You don't need us on-site. We specialize in processing raw survey data (Point Numbers, Easting, Northing, Elevation) provided by clients across the USA, UK, and GCC.

Using AutoCAD Civil 3D, Global Mapper, and Bluebeam Revu, we convert your CSVs, KMLs, and PDFs into final execution files, Volume Reports, and detailed BoQ Sheets.

We provide high-quality sample portfolios before starting, ensuring you get exact precision and professional deliverables.

Our Remote Drafting Solutions

Topographic & Contour Maps

Importing your CSV/Excel point data into Civil 3D to generate precise 1m/0.5m contour lines and finished DWG/PDF maps.

Alignments & Profiles

Drawing road and pipeline alignments. Generating longitudinal profile views showing Natural Ground Level (NGL) vs Design Levels.

Earthwork Cut & Fill

Using Civil 3D Volume Dashboard to compare Original Ground with Excavation surfaces, delivering complete volume reports.

Global Mapper & GIS

Converting Google Earth KML/DEM data to 3D CAD contours and transforming coordinate systems (e.g., UTM to local grids).

Bluebeam PDF Takeoffs

Accurate measurement of lengths and areas from PDF drawings to prepare comprehensive Bill of Quantities (BoQ) sheets.

As-Built Markups

Professionally updating your site red-line markups into final, polished PDF and CAD drawings for final submission.
