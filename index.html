Company Overview

About City Geo Survey

City Geo Survey is a premier land surveying and spatial engineering firm operating in Saudi Arabia. We specialize in translating complex civil engineering designs into pinpoint ground reality.

Equipped with multi-frequency GNSS receivers, high-precision robotic Total Stations, and advanced Civil 3D spatial software, we ensure that infrastructure projects meet strict international tolerance standards.
