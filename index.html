About City Geo Survey

City Geo Survey is a premier land surveying and civil engineering firm providing high-precision spatial solutions across Saudi Arabia.

We leverage advanced surveying instruments and civil design software to execute complex topographic mapping, highway alignments, pipeline quality audits, and structural positioning.

Our Core Services

Civil Infrastructure

Precise site establishment, structural grid layout, benchmark transfer, and excavation control.

Road & Highway Survey

Centerline setting, profile leveling, cross-sections, and earthwork volume calculation.

Pipeline & Utilities

Route alignment, trench excavation checks, sand bedding leveling, and backfilling checks.

Transmission Lines (OHTL)

High-voltage line routing, tower spot positioning, and angle structure coordinate marking.

Building Construction

Column positioning, wall alignment, verticality control, and multi-story floor level checks.

Water & Sewerage Networks

Manhole coordinate setting, gravity pipeline slope establishment, and invert level checks.

Software Arsenal: AutoCAD Civil 3D, Bluebeam Revu, Global Mapper, and Google Earth Pro.
